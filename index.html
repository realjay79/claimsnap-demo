How it works

From the first ladder climb to the depreciation check.

One claim, two packages, one adjuster. ClaimSnap handles the paperwork in between.

1

Shoot with a guided list

Every elevation, every slope, test squares, soft metals and flashing. The app prompts each shot and stamps it, so nothing depends on memory at the top of a ladder.

  • GPS, time and photographer on every photo
  • Speak a note in English or Spanish; it becomes the caption
  • Quick-shoot mode when the crew needs speed; sort later
2

Check it before the adjuster does

Adjusters push back on incomplete or inaccurate documentation. ClaimSnap won't send until every required shot is there, every photo is at the right address after the storm date, and the claim details match.

  • Missing shots named, with one tap to go shoot them
  • Location and date checks on every photo
3

Email the adjuster a file they can read

One tap sends a branded PDF and a no-login gallery link from your own address. Photos are grouped the way an adjuster reviews a roof, and originals download with location data intact.

  • Sectioned by elevation and slope, captioned, stamped
  • Supplements go out the same way, with photo proof per item
4

Close out and get released

Completion photos by stage, an invoice and a signed certificate of completion go to the same adjuster. The claim record shows what was sent, when, and when the adjuster opened it.

  • Homeowner signs on your phone, on site
  • Automatic reminders while depreciation is pending

Questions

Good to know

Does the adjuster need an account?

No. They get an email from your address with the PDF attached and a gallery link that opens in any browser. Links expire after 90 days.

Can my crew keep using the photo app they already have?

Yes. Import photos from the camera roll — they keep their original GPS and time — and ClaimSnap sorts them into the claim file and runs the same accuracy check.

Who can see our photos?

Only your company and the people you send a package to. Photos are stored privately, shared through expiring links, and every send and open is logged.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your plan starts billing monthly on day 15. Cancel from Settings before then and you won't be charged. You can change plans anytime.

Does it work for commercial roofs?

The first release is built for residential insurance claims. Multi-building commercial jobs are on the roadmap — talk to us if that's you.
